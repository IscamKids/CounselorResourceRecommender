Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some of the most frequently asked questions about the counselor recommendation survey. Click on a question to see the answer.

How long does the survey take?
  • The survey typically takes about 5-10 minutes to complete. Once you finish, you will get a wide variety of recommendations personalized for you.

How many recommendation options are there?
  • There are about 20 counseling recommendations available based on your survey responses.

Do I need a password to access the survey?
  • No, you do not need a password to access the survey. You can access it directly from the main page.

Can I save my recommendations?
  • Yes, you can save your recommendations for future reference. This allows you to easily access and review them at any time.

Who can see my saved recommendations?
  • Your saved recommendations can be viewed by you, your parents, and your counselor.

Can I filter out options I don't want?
  • Yes, you are able to filter out options you are not interested in by finding the filter button and entering your preferences.

How do I access my counselor?
  • You can either click the link at the bottom of this page or click this link: Counselors.
